The Bonavista experience

Made for memorable moments.

Bonavista Colombo brings together a relaxed restaurant atmosphere, generous food and an evening experience designed for friends, families and celebrations.

From seafood and Asian-inspired plates to shared dishes and private karaoke moments, the space is built around one simple idea: stay a little longer.

“Come for the food. Stay for the atmosphere.”
